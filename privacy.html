Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Who we are

Jesus Empowerment is the personal brand of Jesus Barreno. The Jesus Empowerment Uploader ("the app") is a private tool he runs on his own computer to publish his own videos.

Who uses the app

Only Jesus Barreno. The app signs in to his own Instagram, Facebook Page, TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign in to it.

What data the app uses

  • Sign-in tokens that Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Google (YouTube) issue for his own accounts.
  • Basic account details such as account name, ID and follower count, used to confirm the right account.
  • The videos, captions and cover images he chooses to post.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and publish his own approved videos and to check that each post went through. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Where the data is kept

Sign-in tokens are stored only on his personal computer, in a folder that only his Windows user can open. No server or database collects them.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in each platform's settings (Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; Facebook and Instagram: Settings → Business Integrations; TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the token files on his computer also ends access.
